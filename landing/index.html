Proof Architecture Roadmap

Trustless training for the People's model.

A Solana-coordinated network where anyone with a GPU joins by posting a bond, earns for gradients that survive verification, and loses the bond if they lie.

The thesis

Frontier AI is produced inside five balance sheets. The mathematics of training over the open internet is already solved and shipping; what remains unsolved is trust. Nobody has made it safe to accept a gradient from a stranger and pay them for it.

Leviathan makes it safe. The chain carries commitments, audits and money. The mesh carries compressed tensors. Verification turns nondeterminism into a priced margin instead of a leap of faith, and a bond turns every identity into a cost. The model belongs to the network that trained it.

Hobbes drew Leviathan as a giant composed of thousands of individuals. This one is composed of thousands of GPUs.

The open slot

Everyone picked one column

Verification without live economics, or economics without verification guarantees. The combination of bonded contributions, random replay audits and slashing is run by nobody for live LLM training. That gap is the whole moat.

The machine

Three layers, each covering the others' gap

Economic security with published parameters, never a cryptographic overclaim.

01 — bound

Robust aggregation

Centered clipping with far-outlier excision caps any contribution's influence in the round it happens. A sign-flip coalition drives naive mean to divergence; clip plus excision holds the honest baseline and admits 3% of malicious deltas.

02 — price

Replay audits

Each contribution is audited with probability p. A local round is a replayable pure function of checkpoint, seed and data, so a mismatch beyond the calibrated tolerance band is a binary fraud proof.

03 — cost

Bonded slashing

Break-even bond = reward × (1−p)/p. A slashed identity re-enters only by posting a fresh bond, and slashed stake funds the verifiers hunting it. Security is self-funding under attack.

Honest limits

What we do not claim

  • This is economic security, not cryptographic proof of training. The uncaught-cheat rate, the tolerance band width, and the bond curve are published, because the honest version is the only durable one.
  • A volunteer-trained model is not yet a frontier model. The claim is a proven path, not an arrived destination; each run scales the trust machine a frontier run will need.
  • Multi-node, multi-week resilience is Phase 2 work. Today's proof is a single machine sustaining real training across epoch boundaries on live devnet.
